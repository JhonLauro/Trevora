1.2.   Scope  
Trevora  is  a  web-based  vehicle  service  history  system  designed  for  vehicle  owners.  The  system  helps  vehicle  
owners
 
capture,
 
validate,

and
 
share
 
vehicle

The  system  allows  vehicle  owners  to  submit  service  records  through  receipt  image  upload,  voice  input,  or  
manual
 
entry.
 
The

extracts
 
or

key
 
service
 
details

as
 
service
 
date,
 
service
 
type,
 
parts
 
replaced,
 
shop/mechanic
 
name,
 
cost,
 
and
 
remarks.
 
Before
 
saving,
 
the
 
owner
 
can
 
review,
 
correct,
 
and
 
confirm
 
the
 
service
 
details.
 
Validated  records  are  stored  under  the  correct  registered  vehicle  profile  and  organized  into  one  chronological  
service
 
history.
 
The

also
 
shows
 
AI-generated
 
explanations
 
beside
 
service
 
records
 
so
 
vehicle
 
owners
 
can
 
understand
 
what
 
was
 
done
 
and
 
why
 
it
 
matters.
 
For  mechanic  handoff,  the  owner  can  generate  a  one-time  QR  access  request  for  the  service  history  of  the  
selected
 
registered
 
vehicle.
 
The

can

past
 
service
 
information.
 
Access

expires

system-set

The  MVP  does  not  include  dealership  integration,  manufacturer  database  integration,  vehicle  diagnostic

2.   Overall  Description 
2.1.   Product  perspective  
Trevora  is  an  independent  web-based  system  for  managing  vehicle  service  history.  It  is  designed  mainly  for  
vehicle
 
owners
 
who
 
collect
 
service
 
records
 
from
 
different
 
repair
 
visits,
 
shops,
 
receipts,
 
voice
 
notes,
 
or
 
manual
 
entries.
 
The
 
system
 
does
 
not
 
require
 
repair
 
shops,
 
dealerships,
 
or
 
manufacturers
 
to
 
use
 
the
 
platform.
 
The  system  has  four  main  modules:  Service  Record  Input,  Service  Data  Validation  and  Correction,  Unified  
Vehicle
 
Service
 
History
 
Consolidation,
 
and
 
AI-Assisted
 
Service
 
Understanding
 
and
 
Mechanic
 
Handoff.
 
These
 
modules
 
help
 
owners
 
capture
 
service
 
information,
 
check
 
details
 
before
 
saving,
 
organize
 
records
 
under
 
the
 
correct
 
vehicle,
 
understand
 
service
 
records
 
through
 
AI-generated
 
explanations,
 
and
 
share
 
service
 
history
 
with
 
mechanics
 
through
 
owner-approved
 
temporary
 
access.
 
Trevora  does  not  connect  to  vehicle  diagnostic  hardware,  dealership  systems,  manufacturer  databases,  or  
third-party
 
repair
 
platforms
 
in
 
the
 
MVP.
 
All
 
service
 
history
 
information
 
depends
 
on
 
the
 
records
 
submitted,
 
reviewed,
 
and
 
confirmed
 
by
 
the
 
vehicle
 
owner.
 
2.2.   User  characteristics  
User  Type  Description  Primary  System  Activities  
Vehicle  Owner  
A  user  who  owns  or  manages  one  or  more  vehicles.  
Upload  receipt  images,  record  voice  input,  enter  service  details  manually,  review  and  correct  service  details,  save  validated  records,  view  service  history,  read  AI-generated  explanations,  generate  QR  access  requests,  and  approve  or  deny  mechanic  access.   
Mechanic  /  Service  Personnel  
A  secondary  user  who  can  request  temporary  access  to  a  vehicle’s  service  history  after  owner  approval.   
Scan  QR  code,  request  access,  wait  for  owner  approval,  view  approved  vehicle  service  history  in  read-only  mode,  and  use  AI-assisted  search  on  shared  records.   
Administrator  
A  user  responsible  for  system  oversight  and  account  support.  
Maintain  accounts,  monitor  system  access,  and  support  data  integrity  controls.

2.4.   Assumptions  and  dependencies  
●
 
Vehicle  owners  are  expected  to  have  access  to  an  internet-enabled  device  and  a  modern  web  
browser.
 
●
 
Receipt  image  extraction  depends  on  the  clarity,  quality,  and  format  of  the  uploaded  receipt  
image.
 
●
 
Speech-to-text  accuracy  depends  on  the  clarity  of  the  audio  input  and  background  noise.  
●
 
AI-generated  explanations  depend  on  the  available  details  inside  validated  service  records.  
●
 
AI-assisted  mechanic  search  depends  on  the  service  history  approved  by  the  vehicle  owner.  
●
 
Vehicle  owners  are  responsible  for  reviewing,  correcting,  and  confirming  service  details  before  
saving.
 
●
 
Mechanics  can  only  access  a  vehicle’s  service  history  after  owner  approval.  
●
 
If  OCR,  speech-to-text,  or  AI  services  are  unavailable,  the  system  shall  still  allow  manual  entry,  
record
 
viewing,
 
and
 
basic
 
service
 
history
 
access
 
when
 
possible.
 
Risk  Impact  Mitigation  
Poor  receipt  image  quality  
Extracted  fields  may  be  inaccurate  or  incomplete.  
The  system  will  show  editable  fields  and  missing-field  warnings.   
Unclear  voice  input   
Transcribed  service  details  may  be  incorrect.   
The  system  will  require  user  review  and  correction  before  saving.   
Wrong  or  incomplete  saved  record   
The  service  history  may  become  unreliable.   
The  system  will  require  validation  and  owner  confirmation  before  saving.   
Incorrect  AI-generated  explanation   
The  owner  may  misunderstand  the  service  record.   
The  system  will  show  the  original  service  record  beside  the  AI  explanation.   
AI  search  returns  unrelated  records   
The  mechanic  may  receive  wrong  or  less  useful  results.   
The  system  will  search  only  approved  vehicle  service  history  and  show  original  record  details.   
Unapproved  mechanic  access   
Vehicle  service  history  privacy  may  be  affected.   
The  system  will  require  owner  approval  before  mechanic  access.   
QR  code  reuse   
Shared  records  may  be  accessed  again  without  permission.   
The  QR  code  will  be  one-time  use  only.   
Expired  access  still  visible   
The  mechanic  may  view  records  longer  than  allowed.   
The  system  will  automatically  expire  access  after  the  system-set  time  limit.   
Internet  connection  problem   
AI  features,  QR  access,  or  cloud  storage  may  not  work  properly.   
The  system  will  allow  manual  entry  and  existing  record  viewing  when  possible.
